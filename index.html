How is the fineness modulus (FM) calculated?細度模數如何計算？

  1. Sum of % passing on all sieves ÷ 100各篩通過百分率總和 ÷ 100
  2. Sum of cumulative % retained on the standard sieves ÷ 100標準篩上累積留篩百分率總和 ÷ 100
  3. Largest size ÷ smallest size最大粒徑 ÷ 最小粒徑
Half-size sieves (12.5, 25 mm) are excluded.半尺寸篩（12.5、25 mm）不計入。

The main drawback of wet sieving is…濕篩最主要的缺點是？

  1. The sample must be dried afterwards量測後樣品必須烘乾
  2. Fines cling to coarser particles細粉附著在粗顆粒上
  3. It needs the refractive index需要已知折射率
Fines clinging is the dry-sieving drawback.細粉附著是乾篩的缺點。

Why are colloids (< 1 μm) hard to measure by gravity sedimentation?為何膠體（< 1 μm）難以用重力沉降量測？

  1. The Reynolds number is too high雷諾數太大
  2. They dissolve它們會溶解
  3. Brownian motion outweighs gravity布朗運動大於重力
Centrifugal sedimentation or photon correlation is used instead.可改用離心沉降或光子相關法。

What does CPFT stand for?CPFT 的意思是？

  1. Cumulative Percent Finer Than累積小於百分率
  2. Coarse Particle Fraction Test粗顆粒比例試驗
  3. Constant Particle Flow Technique定速顆粒流技術
Sieve "% passing" is a CPFT.篩分析的「% 通過」就是 CPFT。

Cu Kα (λ = 0.15418 nm) on quartz (101), d = 0.334 nm. 2θ is about…Cu Kα（λ = 0.15418 nm）照射石英 (101)，d = 0.334 nm，2θ 約為？

  1. 13.3°
  2. 26.7°
  3. 53.4°
sinθ = 0.231, θ = 13.3°, so 2θ = 26.7°.sinθ = 0.231，θ = 13.3°，所以 2θ = 26.7°。

Which reflections appear for a body-centred (I) lattice?體心 I 晶格會出現哪些反射？

  1. All (hkl)全部 (hkl)
  2. h, k, l all odd or all evenh、k、l 全奇或全偶
  3. h + k + l evenh + k + l 為偶數
That is why α-Fe shows (110) but not (100).所以 α-Fe 有 (110) 峰，卻沒有 (100) 峰。

CH (001) is far stronger than the card and the other lines too weak. Most likely cause?CH (001) 遠高於卡片、其他譜線偏低，最可能的原因是？

  1. Preferred orientation of platy crystals片狀晶粒的擇優取向
  2. A 2θ zero offset2θ 零點偏移
  3. Kβ ghost peaksKβ 假峰
Side-loading or spinning the sample reduces it.改用側向裝填或旋轉試樣可以改善。

What does an amorphous (glass) XRD pattern look like?非晶質（玻璃）的 XRD 圖譜長什麼樣？

  1. A set of sharp peaks一組尖銳峰
  2. One broad hump, no sharp peaks一個寬鼓包，沒有尖峰
  3. No signal at all完全沒有訊號
Only short-range order: no sharp constructive interference.只有短程有序，無法在特定角度建設性干涉。

In a BSE image, which phase appears brightest?在背向散射電子（BSE）影像中，哪一種相最亮？

  1. The phase with the highest mean atomic number平均原子序最高的相
  2. The phase with the roughest surface表面最粗糙的相
  3. The phase with the lowest density of pores孔隙最少的相
Backscatter yield rises with atomic number: atomic-number (Z) contrast.背向散射產率隨原子序增加，這就是原子序對比。

Why do edges look bright in a secondary-electron (SE) image?為什麼二次電子（SE）影像中邊緣特別亮？

  1. Edges contain heavier elements邊緣含較重的元素
  2. Edges are charged邊緣帶電
  3. More secondary electrons escape near an edge邊緣附近有更多二次電子逃逸出來
SE come from a shallow depth; near edges more of them can escape.二次電子來自很淺的深度，邊緣附近更容易逃逸。

Raising the accelerating voltage makes the interaction volume…提高加速電壓，交互作用體積會？

  1. Smaller變小
  2. Larger and deeper變大、變深
  3. Unchanged不變
Faster electrons penetrate deeper before losing their energy.電子能量越高，損失能量前穿透得越深。

A non-conductive cement specimen keeps "charging" in the SEM. A common fix?不導電的水泥試片在 SEM 中一直「充電」，常見的解決方法是？

  1. Coat it with a thin conductive layer (e.g. carbon)鍍上一層薄導電膜（例如碳）
  2. Raise the magnification提高放大倍率
  3. Use a thicker specimen改用更厚的試片
Carbon coating is preferred when EDS analysis follows.若之後要做 EDS，常用碳鍍膜。
